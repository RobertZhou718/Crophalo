# The Earth on the home

Two WebP pictures of NASA's Blue Marble, kept in the app so the home never waits for another server.

| File | Size | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| small.webp | 512 x 256, about 9.6 KB | shows at once |
| sharp.webp | 2048 x 1024, about 256 KB | follows, and comes in over the small one in 300 ms (not at Less and Still) |

Source: NASA Earth Observatory's Blue Marble Next Generation (Terra MODIS, 2004), as NASA GIBS serves it:
https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/wms/epsg4326/best/wms.cgi?SERVICE=WMS&REQUEST=GetMap&VERSION=1.3.0&LAYERS=BlueMarble_NextGeneration&STYLES=&CRS=EPSG:4326&BBOX=-90,-180,90,180&WIDTH=2048&HEIGHT=1024&FORMAT=image/jpeg
Fetched on 15 November 2026 (a 2048 x 1024 JPEG, 209,456 bytes), then re-encoded as WebP with ImageMagick: the small one resized to 512 x 256 (quality 72), the sharp one at its own size (quality 90). Nothing was painted or added.

This is NASA imagery. NASA's media guidelines allow it to be used with credit; the home says "Blue Marble (2004): NASA Earth Observatory." Crophalo is not made, reviewed or endorsed by NASA, ESA or NOAA.
