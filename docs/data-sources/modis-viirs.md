## What it is

MODIS (on NASA's Terra and Aqua satellites, since 2000 and 2002) and VIIRS (on Suomi NPP, NOAA-20 and NOAA-21, since 2012) photograph the whole Earth every day at 250 m to 1 km; from them NASA makes NDVI / EVI greenness every 16 days, leaf area, land temperature, evaporation, snow cover and fire hot spots.
They are too coarse for one small field but give 25 years of "when is this land green" and same-day pictures and fire alerts. Land products are held by LP DAAC; near-real-time copies by LANCE; fire hot spots by FIRMS.

Status of the satellites on 2026-11-14 (checked): Terra and Aqua MODIS still deliver (LANCE near-real-time `MOD09GA` and `MYD09GA` files dated 2026-11-13). Suomi NPP VIIRS has stopped: newest `VNP09GA` file is 2026-11-02, every S-NPP layer in GIBS ends 2026-11-02 or 2026-11-03, the FIRMS S-NPP 24-hour file is empty, and the FIRMS FAQ says delivery "ceased at 15:40 Universal Time (UTC) on November 2, 2026" (read). NOAA-20 and NOAA-21 VIIRS deliver.

4. The GIBS layer `MODIS_Terra_NDVI_8Day` (Crophalo's "Plant greenness" layer, described in Crophalo's plan as "from 2000-02-18, 8-day step") now only holds 2025-02-12 to 2026-11-13 in daily steps; a 2010 date answers 404. The long record is in `MODIS_Terra_L3_NDVI_16Day` (from 2000-03-05).

6. FIRMS `country` endpoints are marked "currently unavailable" on the API page (read).

## Recommendation

For greenness history, use the Planetary Computer point read of `modis-13Q1-061` as the fast first route and keep ORNL as the fallback (or AppEEARS with the NASA Earthdata login for the whole 25-year series in one task, which also gives VIIRS `VJ113A1` / `VJ213A1` as the continuation after MODIS); for fires, drop the empty S-NPP file and read NOAA-20 + NOAA-21 (the Canada / USA regional files are 200 times smaller than the world file).
Show the farmer the same "when is the land green" chart and land history, the fire line, and switch the map's long-history greenness to `MODIS_Terra_L3_NDVI_16Day` while keeping the rolling 8-day layer for "now".

## How Crophalo uses it

- Crophalo plans to read ORNL `MOD13Q1` NDVI subsets: the last full year in 3 calls, and every season since 2001 in about 51 calls (110 s timeout, 3 tries); its admin panel checks `/MOD13Q1/dates`.
