## What it is

ECOSTRESS is a NASA/JPL heat camera on the International Space Station. It measures the temperature of the ground and of plants in squares about 70 m wide, between about 52 N and 52 S, at irregular times of day and night (the station's orbit), and NASA turns that into how much water the land is giving off (evapotranspiration, ET), a plant-stress index and water-use efficiency.
Every number file sits at NASA's LP DAAC behind the free Earthdata login; the catalogue, the product lists and the preview pictures need no login. It is still producing data (newest pass over Barrie: 2026-11-10, checked).

## Problems

- **Two versions run side by side.** Version 003 of the tiled LST has almost twice as many files as version 002 for the same 12 months (433 against 232 at Barrie), because, in NASA's words, the version-3 algorithms "run on all pixels regardless of land, ocean, or cloud". Crophalo's planned reader takes "the newest version from CMR", so it now gets 003: more cloudy files to open inside its 115-second budget, and the cloud layer matters more than before. Version-3 file names also drop the build number (`ECOv003_..._01_LST.tif` against `ECOv002_..._0713_01_LST.tif`).
- AppEEARS still offers only version 002 of every ECOSTRESS product, so AppEEARS and a direct read can disagree.
- Every number file answers 302 to the Earthdata login. Without the login the only thing readable is the catalogue and the preview pictures.
- The ET products arrive only for daytime passes and later than the temperature: newest ET over Barrie is 2026-10-21, while the newest temperature is 2026-11-10 (checked). 76 ET passes in 12 months, many of them cloudy.
- disALEXI ET is United States only (none in Ontario, 0 passes at Barrie ever) and nine months stale at Ames.
- No ECOSTRESS in GIBS/Worldview or Planetary Computer; Harmony cannot cut a box out of it and only outputs pictures (JPEG/PNG) for the tiled LST.
- Passes come at any hour and with gaps of days to weeks (the space station's orbit); nothing north of about 52-54 N.

## Recommendation

Keep the planned route (CMR by point, then window reads of the tiled GeoTIFFs with the Earthdata login), pin the version on purpose (003, with its cloud layer) rather than "newest", and add the `ETdaily` and `ETuncertainty` layers of ECO_L3T_JET.003 from the same passes.
Show the farmer two things: how hot the bare ground got on clear days (as planned) and how much water the field gave off on each clear day (mm a day, 70 m squares) against the rain, with the count of clear looks; without the login, show NASA's preview picture of the latest pass and say the numbers need the login.

## How Crophalo uses it

- Crophalo plans to use ECO_L2T_LSTE (the newest version from CMR): it searches the last 365 days by point, reads a 3 x 3 window of `_LST.tif` where `_cloud == 0`, `_water != 1` and `_QC & 3 != 3`, uses day passes only, and shows the card "Ground surface temperature" (ground at 45 C or more on N of M clear days).
- Not used yet: evapotranspiration (L3T_JET), evaporative stress index (L4T_ESI), water-use efficiency (L4T_WUE), downscaled soil moisture (L3T_SM), the no-login preview pictures, AppEEARS.
