## What it is

NLDAS-2 (North America, 1/8 degree about 12 km, hourly, 1979 to about 3 days ago) and GLDAS-2 (whole world, 1/4 degree about 25 km, 3-hourly, 2000 to 1.5-3.5 months ago) are NASA land models fed with weather: they give rain, air temperature, soil moisture in layers, soil temperature, evaporation, snow and runoff for every grid square.
They are model output, not a measurement of the field: one number per 12 km (NLDAS) or 25 km (GLDAS) square.

Coverage (checked in the catalogue answer): the NLDAS-2 box is lon -125 to -67, lat 25 to 53, so Barrie, Ontario (44.40, -79.78) and Ames, Iowa are both inside. GLDAS is global. NLDAS-2 rain in Canada is weaker than in the USA (I think: gauge analysis is USA-only, Canada falls back to the NARR reanalysis; the NLDAS-2 README says this, not re-read).

## Problems

1. **Data Rods is gone.** Every Data Rods request answers 301 to a GES DISC alert page whose title says the service "will be discontinued no earlier than October 31, 2025 and transitioned to a new data access method". The challenge resource list still names it. Old tutorials and the catalogue's own link "Hydrology Time Series" still point to it.
2. **The old GES DISC OPeNDAP on hydro1 is gone**: 410 "Service Permanently Retired", page dated 17 August 2026. It names Earthdata Cloud OPeNDAP as the replacement.
3. **The GrADS Data Server is gone**: 410, page dated 17 August 2026.
4. Everything that returns numbers now sits behind the NASA Earthdata login (Giovanni API, cloud OPeNDAP, HTTPS files, S3, Harmony). Without the login the only working routes are the catalogue (dates and file names) and the GIBS monthly pictures.
5. The GES DISC alert page itself is a script-only page: its text could not be read by a plain fetch, so the name of the "new data access method" is taken from an Earthdata forum reply (Giovanni time-series API), not from the alert.
6. A NASA alert (2 April 2026, read) says the new Giovanni time-series service returned "small spurious blocks of zeros" for NLDAS/GLDAS variables for data up to 1 January 2024; fixed 26 March 2026. A zero from this service should be cross-checked.
7. GLDAS is late: the main GLDAS-2.1 Noah product ends 31 July 2026 (3.5 months behind); the early product ends 15 October 2026 (1 month behind). NLDAS-2 is 3 days behind.
8. Catalogue oddity: the "metadata" links in the granule answers point to unrelated Earthdata catalogue pages (`.../catalog/ges-disc-cms-go-ch4-sec-tdyc-na-1`, `.../ges-disc-omto3d-004`).
9. NLDAS-3 on AWS is beta, 2001-2023 only, 700 MB per daily file: not usable for a live point lookup yet.
10. Side point for Crophalo (not checked here): Crophalo's planned IMERG rain read uses a `gpm1.gesdisc.eosdis.nasa.gov/opendap/...` address in its example. Since the hydro1 on-premises OPeNDAP now answers 410, the gpm1 one should be checked (see [gpm-imerg.md](gpm-imerg.md): it still answered 302 to the login).

## Recommendation

Without a login, add the six GIBS monthly layers (NLDAS/GLDAS soil moisture, rain, temperature) as map pictures: "soil moisture across your region this month versus other years", 1979 onward for southern Ontario and the USA.
With the NASA Earthdata login, call the Giovanni time-series API (one call per variable) for the field's 12 km NLDAS square: 45 years of root-zone soil moisture, evaporation and snow, to show "how wet is the soil now compared with the same week in past years"; use GLDAS only outside the NLDAS box.

## How Crophalo uses it

Crophalo does not use it yet. For water in the soil and rain, Crophalo uses or plans to use SMAP (9 km soil moisture, behind the Earthdata login, plus the SMAP GIBS layer), GRACE-FO, IMERG rain (CMR + GES DISC OPeNDAP) and NASA POWER; it does not use NLDAS, GLDAS, Data Rods or Giovanni.
