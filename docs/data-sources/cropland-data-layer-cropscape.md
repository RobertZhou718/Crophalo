## What it is

USDA NASS's yearly satellite map of which crop grew on every pixel of US farmland (48 lower states since 2008, some states back to 1997; 30 m, and 10 m from 2024; Hawaii separately).
CropScape (George Mason University) is the old web app and web service for it; CroplandCROS (USDA SCINet) is the current official app, with ArcGIS image services behind it.

## Where the second checks differed

- CropScape GetCDLValue: second check A got all four answers in about 2 s; second check B got three 60 s timeouts with 0 bytes. The first check saw timeouts and 40 s. The service is unstable across the day; treat it as unreliable.
- CropScape WSDL: 200, 0.7 s, 26.5 kB in second check A (first check: timeout).
- CropScape WMS: 4.8 s and 2.0 s in A, 29.7 s in B (first check 14-16 s). Works, at very different speeds.
- CropScape GetCDLFile: the bbox form hung, but the documentation's `fips` example answered in 4.8 s. GetCDLStat was down for both the given request and the documentation's own example.
- CroplandCROS identify: one call with `returnCatalogItems` returns the whole crop history of the point, with the raster names.
- AAFC Annual Crop Inventory is not broken: the guessed addresses were wrong. The address in the open.canada.ca record works without a key.

## Terms

- NASS metadata/FAQ page `https://www.nass.usda.gov/research-and-science/cropland/sarsfaqs2` (**read**): access constraints "none"; the Cropland Data Layer and the data on CroplandCROS are "considered public domain and free to redistribute"; NASS "does not warrant any conclusions drawn from these data".
- Free public site: **yes**. Business: **yes** (public domain, read).
- Credit line (**read**, metadata form): "United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) National Agricultural Statistics Service (NASS), 20260227, Cropland Data Layer: USDA NASS, USDA NASS Marketing and Information Services Office, Washington, D.C.", with the link `https://croplandcros.scinet.usda.gov/`.
- Rate limits: none published for the SCINet image services (not found); none read for the GMU service (its home page now redirects to a sign-in). The SCINet service is the back end of an official app, not an advertised public API: call it gently, cache for a long time, and ask NASS (the page names "USDA NASS Customer Service") before heavy use (I think).
- Planetary Computer terms and limits: not read.
- AAFC Annual Crop Inventory: Open Government Licence - Canada (read in the open.canada.ca record in second check B).

## Problems

- **CropScape's web service is unreliable.** In the first check, of 7 calls to `CDLService`, 5 hung for 60 s with no answer (GetCDLValue twice, GetCDLStat twice, GetCDLFile once), one took 40 s, and only the "bad year" refusal came back quickly (7 s). The WSDL also timed out. Second check A got fast answers; second check B got three timeouts. Crophalo's plan of about 90 GetCDLValue calls with a 25 s limit would fail on a bad hour.
- **The CropScape website now redirects to a "CropSmart" sign-in page**, so the "USDA CropScape (crops by field)" link in Crophalo's parcel look-up list no longer opens the map.
- The service now says years run **1997 to 2025** (Crophalo's text says from 2008). Before 2008 only some states were mapped: at the Iowa test spot 2000 to 2007 have values and 1999 is NoData.
- The CDL is **10 m from 2024** (read in the NASS FAQ); Crophalo's source text says "every 30 m square".
- NASS moved its files: the old `/Research_and_Science/Cropland/Release/datasets/...` path answers 404; files now sit under `/sites/default/files/...`.
- The current NASS FAQ and release pages name CroplandCROS, not CropScape, as the place to get the data (CropScape is mentioned once, as history).
- Planetary Computer's copy stops at 2021.
- **Ontario is not covered by any CDL route** (US only). Canada's equivalent is the AAFC Annual Crop Inventory; the first check's guessed addresses gave 404, but second check B found the documented address, which works without a key.

## Recommendation

Move Crophalo's crop-history lookup to the SCINet service: one `CDL_WM/ImageServer/identify?...returnCatalogItems=true` call per spot (0.3 s) gives every year at once, instead of about 90 slow CropScape calls; keep CropScape only as a fall-back. Show the farmer the year-by-year crop strip ("corn, soybeans, corn...") plus "corn 11 of the last 18 years" from the frequency layers, and point the US link at CroplandCROS. For Ontario, the AAFC Annual Crop Inventory `identify` gives the same kind of history.

## How Crophalo uses it

- Crophalo already calls CropScape `GetCDLValue` once per spot and year (up to 5 spots, 2008 to the newest year, 6 calls at once, 25 s limit, 2 tries), only in the 48 lower states, when the farmer taps the button. That is the route that hung in second check B. "USDA CropScape" is also a link in Crophalo's parcel look-up list.
- Not used yet: the SCINet image services (CroplandCROS `identify` takes latitude and longitude directly and returns every year in one answer, so it is a ready second source), the frequency and cultivated layers, GetCDLStat/GetCDLFile, the WMS, the national files, Crop Sequence Boundaries, Planetary Computer, the confidence layer, and the AAFC Annual Crop Inventory for Ontario.
