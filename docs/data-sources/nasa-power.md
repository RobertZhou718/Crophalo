## What it is

NASA POWER (Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources, NASA Langley) serves ready-made daily, hourly, monthly and long-term weather and sunshine for any point on Earth from 1981, on a grid about 50 km wide (0.5 x 0.625 degrees), from NASA's MERRA-2 / GEOS-IT weather models, CERES sunshine and GPM IMERG rain.
It has three "communities" (AG agroclimatology, RE renewable energy, SB sustainable buildings) which are the same data in different units and file formats. No key, no account.

## Where the second check differed

1. Climate zones: 200 but **15.3 s** (first check 1.7 s). The answer's own timer says 1.1 s of data work.
2. Climate indicators: 200 but **17.8 s** (first check 8.7 s).
3. Regional insights: the documentation shows a **GET** with `geometry=`, not a POST. Its first example, tried exactly, gave a 404 HTML page: the service is not answering at its documented address.
4. `IMERG_PRECTOT`: empty (-999) on all 44 days at Barrie for 1 Oct - 13 Nov 2026 (same as the first check), but **it does return numbers for June 2024** at Barrie and at Ames (two extra calls). So it is late, not dead. The bucket's `last_available.json` says imerg daily reaches 2026-11-12, which does not match what the API returns.
5. `RZMC`/`SFMC` asked alone for June 2024 at Barrie: all -999 and `"sources": []`. Confirmed empty; not a side effect of mixing parameters.
6. OPeNDAP: the old host answers 301 to an Earthdata page (not followed).
7. `last_available.json` lists "smap" but the bucket has no smap/ folder; it has gwm/, which the file does not list.

## Terms

- Licence: the AWS bucket carries `LICENSE.txt` = Creative Commons **Attribution 4.0 International** (checked: file header read). The POWER site's own page asks for a reference, not a fee. NASA data policy is open; no POWER page that forbids business use was found (not read: no such page found).
- Free public site: yes. Business: yes under CC BY 4.0 (attribution) (read).
- Credit line asked for (read, `power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/referencing/`): "The data was obtained from National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) Langley Research Center's Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources (POWER) project funded through the NASA Earth Science Division", plus the service name, version and the date accessed (for example "POWER Project's Daily 2.10.3 version on 2026/11/14"). They also ask to be told by email when the data is passed on (the address is on that referencing page).
- Rate limits: the docs list 429 "Too Many Requests" and warn that an application that keeps asking for the same grid cell "will potentially be blocked" (read). **An exact number per minute: not found (not read).** Limits read: regional requests take one parameter; a point request takes up to 20 parameters (I think; not re-read). The AWS bucket has no such limit.

## Problems

- `IMERG_PRECTOT` (satellite rain) is **-999 on every day after 2026-09-14** at Barrie (checked: 257 valid of 317 days of 2026), although POWER's own `last_available.json` says IMERG daily reaches 2026-11-12. So the two-month lag in the API is real, and the fresher IMERG that POWER holds in its bucket is not served under this parameter. Crophalo's own IMERG fill (max 60 days) just reaches: 14 Sep to 13 Nov is 60 days. One more day of lag and the fill leaves a hole. (The second check found numbers for June 2024: the parameter is late, not dead.)
- `RZMC`, `RZMC_PRCNTL`, `SFMC` (root-zone and surface soil moisture, m3/m3) and `GWM_HEIGHT_ANOMALY` are in the AG daily catalogue but returned **-999 on every day** at Barrie (2025-01-01..2026-11-13) and at Ames (2023-2024), checked in both checks. The answer carries the message "GWM Data Site: No GWM observation sites within 50 miles." Do not plan on them.
- Sunshine parameters (`ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN`, PAR) asked with `time-standard=UTC` end 2026-08-31 at Barrie (checked; source SYN1DEG only), about 10 weeks behind. The same parameter asked without a time standard (so in local solar time, RE community, at Ames) had values to 2026-11-05 from FLASHFlux (checked). I think the cause is the time standard, not the community: `last_available.json` lists FLASHFlux in LST only. Worth knowing if daily sunshine is ever shown: UTC loses the newest ten weeks.
- `/api/temporal/climatology/global` answers 404 (the global endpoints are gone from the specs). OPeNDAP is decommissioned. `/api/application/insights/docs` 404, and the documented insights example also 404 (second check).
- Monthly and climatology answers are in local solar time (LST) even though daily was asked in UTC: `time_standard: "LST"` in their headers (checked). Days and months therefore do not line up to the hour.
- The MERRA-2 part of the record ends 2026-10-01; newer days are GEOS-IT (the header lists `GEOSIT`), which NASA later replaces. The newest six weeks can shift a little when re-read.
- The climate indicators report took 8.7 s (17.8 s in the second check); the validate report 5.5 s and 123 kB; climate zones took 15.3 s in the second check. These are too slow and heavy for a page load.

## Recommendation

Keep the daily point API with `community=AG` as the backbone (as Crophalo does now), and add from the same free service: soil temperature (`TSOIL1`) for "is the seedbed warm enough to sow", growing degree days and dew point/humidity/wind for disease and spray windows, and the projection API for a "your field in the 2040s" card.
Use `last_available.json` from the bucket for the honest "weather through <date>" line per source, and do not use RZMC/SFMC (empty) or the global/OPeNDAP routes (gone).

## How Crophalo uses it

- Crophalo already uses the daily point API, AG community, with six parameters (T2M_MAX, T2M_MIN, PRECTOTCORR, IMERG_PRECTOT, GWETROOT, EVLAND) from 2001-01-01 to yesterday in UTC. It uses the cell height for a lapse-rate fix, and takes IMERG rain first and MERRA-2 rain as the fallback. It plans to fill the newest weeks with IMERG Late/Early files from GES DISC (this fill needs the NASA Earthdata login; see [gpm-imerg.md](gpm-imerg.md)). It also uses the climatology point for direct sunshine (ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DNI, CLRSKY_SFC_SW_DNI).
- Not used yet: hourly, monthly, regional, wind rose, indicators, projections, validate, soil temperature, degree days, the AWS Zarr bucket.
