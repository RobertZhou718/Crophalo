## What it is

NASS is USDA's statistics service. QuickStats holds its published figures (acres, yield per county, prices received, crop progress and condition, the Census of Agriculture); the Cropland Data Layer (CDL) is its yearly 30 m (and now 10 m) map of which crop grew on every US field.
Everything is United States only: the 48 lower states for the CDL, Hawaii separately. Nothing for Ontario.

## Where the second check differed

1. **CropScape GetCDLValue hung**: three calls (Iowa 2024, Iowa 2025, Ontario point) each timed out at 60 s with 0 bytes. The first check saw 1.2 s. The same host was slow all round (WMS capabilities 29.7 s, the CropScape home redirect 18.3 s). Crophalo already depends on this route, so treat it as unreliable.
2. **CropScape WMS**: 200 but 29.7 s, not 1.2 s. Works, slowly.
3. **AAFC Annual Crop Inventory is not broken; the address was wrong.** The two guessed addresses are 404 in both checks, but the address in the open.canada.ca record works without a key, and `identify` at Barrie (44.40, -79.78) answered value 122 = "Pasture/forages" in AAFC's own class table. Years 2009 to 2025 are listed. This is the Ontario twin of the US crop map.
4. The CDL release page at `.../Release/index.php` first answers 301 to `/research-and-science/cropland/release`, which is the 200 page.
5. CroplandCROS identify returns one value per year raster in `properties.Values`: one call gives the whole crop history of a point.

## Terms

- QuickStats API: "NASS API Terms of Service" on `https://quickstats.nass.usda.gov/api` (read). Use to search, display, analyse and retrieve is allowed; the line **"This product uses the NASS API but is not endorsed or certified by NASS."** must be shown prominently; the NASS name may identify the source but not imply endorsement of any product "not-for-profit, commercial or otherwise"; content may not be modified and still credited to NASS. So a free public site may use it, and a business may (read).
- Limits: 50,000 records per request, checked first with `get_counts` (read). No calls-per-minute figure is published; NASS reserves the "Right to Limit" and may block a key (read).
- CDL: public domain, credit "USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service Cropland Data Layer, {year}" (I think; the CDL metadata page was not read for this resource; see [cropland-data-layer-cropscape.md](cropland-data-layer-cropscape.md), where it was read). The Planetary Computer collection labels its licence "proprietary" with a link to NASS's terms (checked the label only).
- CroplandCROS services: no terms text on the service itself (`copyrightText` is empty, checked).
- AAFC Annual Crop Inventory: Open Government Licence - Canada (read in the open.canada.ca record, second check).

## Problems

- The CropScape, VegScape and Crop-CASMA web sites now redirect to a "CropSmart" sign-in at George Mason University. The CDL web service Crophalo uses (`/axis2/services/CDLService/GetCDLValue`) still answered without a login in the first check, but it lives on the same host: it could go behind the same login. In the second check it timed out on every call.
- `https://www.nass.usda.gov/datasets/` (with the final slash) redirects to `http://www-origin.nass.usda.gov/datasets`, an internal-looking plain-http address; without the slash it works. Pages also name `www-origin` as their canonical address.
- CDL download links moved: the old `/Research_and_Science/Cropland/Release/datasets/…zip` path is 404.
- Planetary Computer's CDL copy stops at 2021.
- No Ontario coverage in any NASS route. The Canadian crop inventory needs its documented address (the guessed one is 404).

## Recommendation

Keep the CDL history button, but point it at USDA's own CroplandCROS ImageServer (`pdi.scinet.usda.gov/image/rest/services/CDL_WM/ImageServer/identify`, keyless, lat/lon in, 0.3 s) with the GMU CropScape service as the back-up, because CropScape's sites went behind a login and its web service hung in the second check. For Ontario, use the AAFC Annual Crop Inventory ImageServer the same way. Add county yields from the keyless QuickStats bulk file (or the API once Crophalo has a free key) to show "farmers in Story County averaged 205 bushels of corn an acre over ten years", with the NASS line; US only.
