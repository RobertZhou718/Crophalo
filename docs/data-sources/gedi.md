## What it is

GEDI is a NASA / University of Maryland laser on the International Space Station. It fires at the ground in spots 25 m wide along thin tracks between 51.6 N and 51.6 S and measures how tall the trees are and how they are layered; from that NASA estimates the wood above ground (biomass, tonnes per hectare).
It is a sample, not a picture: most fields were never hit. The gridded products (L3 height, L4B biomass) average the spots into 1 km squares. Footprint files are at NASA's LP DAAC (L1B, L2A, L2B) and ORNL DAAC (L3, L4A, L4B, L4C), all behind the free Earthdata login.

## Routes

## Problems

- Every number file (L4B, L3, L4A, L2A/B) answers 302 to the Earthdata login. With no login only the catalogue and the GIBS map pictures work.
- **L4B biomass is frozen at March 2023** (collection marked COMPLETE, version 2.1, no version 3), while the height products have moved to version 3 with data to July 2025 and footprints into 2026. Crophalo's biomass figure is a 2019-2023 average and should say so.
- Version 002 of L1B/L2A/L2B ended 2025-07-10; version 003 (released June 2026) is the live one and its granules are quarter orbits of 1.0-1.7 GB. Code or documents that name "GEDI02_A.002" are out of date.
- The newest footprint granule anywhere is 2026-06-10 and the newest over Barrie is 2026-03-14: five to eight months behind 2026-11-14. Whether the instrument is still collecting was not confirmed.
- `webmap.ornl.gov` (ORNL's map and cut-out service) does not resolve; the `daac.ornl.gov` dataset pages redirect to earthdata.nasa.gov. Old ORNL links in tutorials are dead or moved.
- The GLAD download link on its own page points to a host that now answers 301 (moved to `gladxfer.umd.edu`). The file is stored in strips, not tiles, so a point read costs a whole compressed row.
- AppEEARS has no GEDI; Planetary Computer has no GEDI; OpenTopography needs a key that may not be built into an app.
- GEDI is a sample: on farmland the 1 km squares often hold few or no shots (around Barrie a quarter of the L4B squares are empty in the GIBS picture), and nothing exists north of 51.6 N (northern Ontario, the Prairies' north).

## Recommendation

Keep the planned L4B window read (mean and standard error, 5 x 5 squares) with the Earthdata login, add the L3 version-3 `rh100_mean` file from the same host for "trees near you average X m tall" (data to July 2025), and say on the card that biomass is the 2019-2023 average.
Without the login, fall back to the GIBS map layer (`GEDI_ISS_L4B_Aboveground_Biomass_Density_Mean_201904-202303`) as a picture under the field; keep the Meta/WRI 1 m map for hedges and shade, since GEDI itself is too sparse for one field.

## How Crophalo uses it

- Crophalo plans to read GEDI L4B gridded biomass (the newest collection matching `GEDI_L4B_Gridded_Biomass*`): the `_MU.tif` and `_SE.tif` files at 1 km, a 5 x 5 window, shown as the card "Tree biomass nearby" in t/ha, nothing beyond 51.6 degrees, kept 90 days.
- Crophalo already uses the Meta/WRI canopy height map (calibrated on GEDI) for shade.
- Not used yet: L3 canopy height, L4A/L2A footprints through Harmony, the GIBS layers, the L4B quality and sample-count files (QF, NS, PE).
